How MoneyMan uses Google data

Google sign-in is optional and is used only for the encrypted backup and restore feature. MoneyMan requests access to its own private Google Drive application-data folder (drive.appdata) so it can upload and restore encrypted MoneyMan backups. It cannot browse the files in your regular Drive.

MoneyMan also reads the selected Google Account's email address so the app can identify and display the backup account on your device. The email address is not sent to the developer, used for advertising, or shared with third parties.

Backup data is encrypted on your device before upload. Google authorization never gates the app's local finance features, and access can be disconnected from MoneyMan Settings or your Google Account at any time.

MoneyMan's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
